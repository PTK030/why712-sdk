Current versions of why712 are source-available under the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0.
Permitted noncommercial use is free.

Commercial use — including use for a business or employer, client work, a paid service, or a
revenue-generating product — requires separate written commercial terms.

Contact PTK030 through the contact information on the
[PTK030 GitHub profile](https://github.com/PTK030). Price and terms are agreed separately.

Copies already received under the project's previous MIT license retain the rights that license
granted. The current license change cannot revoke those existing grants.

Read the repository's [license text](https://github.com/PTK030/why712/blob/main/LICENSE) and
[commercial licensing summary](https://github.com/PTK030/why712/blob/main/COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md).
This page is not legal advice.
